The joined strips are six neighbouring residues, but which six? Residue types pin them to the sequence. Types can come from other spectra (such as the Cβ shifts of an HNCACB, or a HADAMAC, which sorts amino acids into classes by their side chains), from unlabelling (a residue type left unlabelled drops out of the spectra), or from chemical shift prediction.

Here four of the six types are known: two Asp and two Ile. The other two (???) could be anything.

Original data and papers

Papers

  • A. P. Kalverda, G. S. Thompson, A. Vogel, M. Schröder, A. G. Bowie, A. R. Khan and S. W. Homans (2009). Poxvirus K7 protein adopts a Bcl-2 fold: biochemical mapping of its interactions with human DEAD box RNA helicase DDX3. J. Mol. Biol. 385, 843–853. doi:10.1016/j.jmb.2008.09.048
    The K7 assignment, its NMR structure and the DDX3 peptide titration: the spectra of steps 1–5, and the shift changes and helices of step 5 (from its Fig. 6).
  • S. Oda, M. Schröder and A. R. Khan (2009). Structural basis for targeting of human RNA helicase DDX3 by poxvirus protein K7. Structure 17, 1528–1537. doi:10.1016/j.str.2009.09.005
    The crystal structure of K7 with the DDX3 peptide (step 5).

Database entries

  • BMRB 15740 (doi:10.13018/BMR15740): K7 backbone and side-chain 1H, 13C and 15N chemical shifts, the HSQC and strips used throughout.
  • PDB 2K36 (doi:10.2210/pdb2K36/pdb): the NMR structure of K7.
  • PDB 3JRV: the crystal structure of K7 with the DDX3 peptide.

How the game's data were made

The scripts in tools/ rebuild every data file in assets/: fetch_bmrb.py (shifts from BMRB 15740), make_hsqc_svg.py (contours), extract_overlay.py (the spectra with and without the peptide, and the minimal shift mapping), digitise_csp_figure.py (Fig. 6 of Kalverda et al.) and extract_1d.py (the 1D spectrum). Python runs in the browser with Pyodide.
