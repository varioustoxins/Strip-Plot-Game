NMR Triple Resonance Strip Plot Game

Learn protein backbone assignment through interactive strip plot puzzles

How to Play

Goal: Connect amino acid residues in the correct sequential order using NMR strip plots.

Understanding Strip Plots:

  • HNCA: Shows peaks for both i (intraresidue) and i-1 (sequential) CA carbons
  • HNCO: Shows the i-1 (sequential) CO carbon
  • Each strip shows the chemical shifts for a specific residue's backbone atoms

Instructions:

  1. Click on a residue strip to select it
  2. Click on another residue to connect them
  3. Match the CA and CO chemical shifts between sequential residues
  4. Continue until all residues are connected in order
  5. Click "Check Answer" to verify your solution

Tip: Look for matching CA(i-1) values in HNCA with CA(i) values in the previous residue!
